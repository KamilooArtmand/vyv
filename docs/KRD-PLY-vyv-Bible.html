01 Executive Summary

One platform for a nation's sound and screen.

Around 35–45 million Kurds live across four states and a global diaspora, yet they have never had one high-quality, well-structured home where they can listen, watch, read and preserve their culture. Today it is scattered across YouTube uploads, unlicensed websites, fading radio archives and private collections. vyv fixes this with a national-grade SuperApp. It is licensed, multi-dialect and available on every platform, and AI runs it end to end.

The proposition

Problem

Kurdish culture is fragmented, unlicensed, poorly tagged and at risk of loss. Artists earn close to nothing. Audiences, especially in KRI, often have no way to pay internationally.

Solution

One licensed SuperApp: music, radio, podcasts, audiobooks with a living book reader, a heritage archive, a wiki, AI-assisted music and a phased video service with Kurdish subtitles.

Moat

The canonical Kurdish media graph: rights, metadata, lyrics, biographies and archives, curated by AI agents and human editors. It is a national asset that cannot be copied.

Why now

  • Digital payments in Iraq are growing fast. E-wallets, digital banks and carrier billing make local micro-payments possible for the first time.
  • Generative and agentic AI turn the cost of metadata, lyrics, subtitles, restoration and support from "armies of staff" into software.
  • Heritage is time-critical. The first generation of recorded Kurdish voices and their custodians is ageing. Tapes and reels degrade every year.
  • Global platforms under-serve Kurdish. They have no dialect-aware search, no Sorani RTL-first UX, no archival depth and no local payments.

The ask. A strategic partnership with the Kurdistan Regional Government and/or top-tier private investors. It funds a fully operational launch on Web and Windows (Microsoft Store) with real sign-in, real licensed catalogue, playlists and in-app purchases, followed by mobile and video. 02 Name & Meaning

vyv: a wave, a wing, a waveform.

vyv is pronounced /weɪv/, like the English "wave". Its three letters are a symbol as much as a word. The two vs are wings in flight around a central y, a rising body. Read together, the letterforms trace the peaks and troughs of an audio waveform.

Wave · شەپۆل · pêl

Sound travels as waves. vyv is the carrier: every voice, song and story moves across it.

Wing · باڵ · bask

A bird in flight is freedom without borders. The mark and wordmark echo open wings, a nation's culture that crosses every frontier.

Flame · ئاگر · agir

The brand accent #ff3c00 is the Newroz fire, the oldest living symbol of Kurdish renewal. It burns as a single dot, the crest of the wave.

Writing the name

  • Always lowercase: vyv, never "VYV" or "Vyv", including at the start of a sentence.
  • In Sorani (Arabic script) text keep the Latin wordmark vyv embedded LTR. Do not transliterate the logo.
  • Tagline in three languages: Sorani, Kurmanji (Latin) and English. Other dialects (Hawrami, Badini, Southern Kurdish, Zazaki) are added per market.

A visual palindrome

Reads the same both ways

vyv is symmetrical, like a bird's wings or a sound wave: it does not change when mirrored. For a nation that writes both right-to-left and left-to-right, a name that is identical in both directions is a wordless statement of unity.

Risk: pronunciation

Many people will read it /vɪv/, not “wave”. Teach it as part of the story (“vyv, say wave”) and repeat it in the sonic logo, or accept both readings. Many great names live with two.

Risk: registration

Three letters may already be registered in Nice classes 9, 38, 41 and 42. Search the WIPO Global Brand Database and secure ply.krd, vyv.krd and the handles krd.ply / krdply before any branding spend.

04 The Problem

A great culture, stored in a broken shelf.

Fragmentation

Content is scattered across YouTube, Telegram, Instagram, personal websites and private hard drives. There is no canonical catalogue and no single source of truth.

Poor metadata

Songs are mislabelled, credits are missing, dialects are untagged and lyrics are unavailable or wrong. Search in Sorani and Kurmanji rarely works.

No rights economy

Piracy is the default. Artists, poets, composers and labels see almost no revenue. There is no functioning collective management organisation for Kurdish music.

Heritage at risk

Reel-to-reel tapes, cassettes, radio archives and dengbêj recordings decay every year. Much of this history exists in a single fragile copy.

Payment exclusion

Many users lack international cards, and subscriptions priced in USD/EUR are unaffordable. The result is exclusion, and exclusion leads to piracy.

Low craft

Existing Kurdish media apps look and feel dated, with weak UX, no personalisation, no offline mode and no premium experience worthy of the culture.

11 vyv Books

Hear it. Read it. At the same time.

Audiobooks ship with a Living Book, a beautifully typeset digital edition rendered natively by the vyv design system instead of a PDF or scanned file. Text and narration are synchronised word-by-word, like sheet music for the spoken word.

Read-along

Built on EPUB 3 Media Overlays (SMIL) and the W3C Audiobooks profile. The current sentence glows, you tap any word to jump the audio there, and you can switch between reading and listening seamlessly.

Typography engine

A real book layout: drop caps, verse layout for poetry (classical dîwan couplets), footnotes, glossary pop-overs, RTL Sorani and LTR Kurmanji, and adjustable size, theme and line height.

Parallel text

Side-by-side original and transliteration or translation. This matters for classical poetry (Nalî, Mahwi, Mawlawi) and for diaspora learners.

12 vyv Heritage

The national sound archive, alive.

دەنگە نەمرەکان · Dengên Nemir, the immortal voices. vyv Heritage collects, restores, catalogues and presents historical recordings: dengbêj epics, early radio broadcasts, speeches, poetry readings, interviews, field recordings and the voices of the figures who shaped Kurdish art and history.

Pipeline: from shoebox to stream

1 · Collect

Donations, family archives, broadcaster archives (e.g. historic Kurdish radio services in Baghdad and Yerevan), universities, collectors. Signed deed of gift or licence.

2 · Digitise

Preservation masters to IASA TC-04: 24-bit/96 kHz Broadcast WAV, checksums (SHA-256), two geographic copies plus cold storage.

3 · Restore

AI-assisted de-noise, de-click, de-hum and speed and pitch correction on access copies only. Masters are never altered.

4 · Describe

Dublin Core + PREMIS metadata, transcription, translation, biography, story of the work, photos and documents. Every claim is sourced.

The archive follows the OAIS reference model (ISO 14721). vyv acts as the access layer; the preservation layer can be operated jointly with a national library or university under public stewardship, so the heritage belongs to the nation, not to a company.

13 vyv Wiki & Timeline

The encyclopedia of Kurdish sound.

A structured, cited, multilingual knowledge base of Kurdish music and spoken-word culture, connected directly to playback. Read about a maqam and hear it. Read a poet's biography and play the songs set to their verses.

Knowledge graph

Entities: Artist, Poet, Composer, Voice Actor, Work, Recording, Album, Instrument, Genre, Maqam, Region, Studio, Label, Broadcaster, Event. Aligned with Wikidata and MusicBrainz identifiers.

Timeline

Year-by-year history of Kurdish music and media, with a draggable ruler, decade jumps, "Sound of the year" and "On this day". This is already built.

Editorial model

AI drafts, human editors verify, and everything is cited. Expert boards per field (musicology, literature, history). Community suggestions go through a moderated queue.

14 vyv Studio

AI-made music, labelled and fair.

A dedicated, clearly-labelled space for AI-assisted and AI-generated Kurdish music and sound, plus creator tools that help human artists produce faster.

Transparency by design

Every AI work carries a visible "AI" badge and C2PA Content Credentials. This aligns with the EU AI Act transparency duties for synthetic content. AI tracks are never disguised as human.

Consent & voice rights

No voice cloning of any person, living or dead, without documented consent from the person or their estate. Revenue shares go to estates when a consented voice model is used.

Creator tools

Stem separation, mastering, lyric assistance in all dialects, maqam-aware melody suggestions, cover-art generation and instrument samples (tembûr, daf, bilûr, duduk, zurna, kemançe).

Separate royalty pool

AI tracks are paid from a separate, capped pool so they never dilute human artists' income.

15 vyv Screen · Video decision

Should vyv do video? Yes, in phases.

Video leads attention today, and investors will ask. We recommend including video in the vision and the demo from day one, but rolling it out in cost-controlled phases. Video costs roughly 10–50× more per user-hour to deliver than audio (bandwidth, storage, DRM, licensing), so audio stays the economic core while video becomes the growth engine.

Ethical access to world cinema

Many Kurdish viewers cannot pay international prices, have no credit cards and live in a culture of free, pirated viewing. vyv offers a legal alternative:

  • Regional licensing at emerging-market rates, negotiated by territory (KRI/Iraq, and per-market for Türkiye, Syria and the diaspora), with AVOD (ad-supported free), SVOD (subscription) and TVOD (single rental at micro-prices) windows.
  • Local payments: IQD wallets, carrier billing and prepaid vouchers sold in shops, so no card is needed.
  • Kurdish subtitles as the core added value. See § Language & Subtitle Stack. This is also a public good for language revitalisation.
  • Anti-piracy: multi-DRM (Widevine, PlayReady, FairPlay), forensic watermarking for premium titles, device limits.

Recommendation: show V1 (Clips + Music Videos) working in the investor demo, and present V2/V3 with a licensing roadmap and letters of intent from Kurdish producers. This signals full ambition without taking on video's cost before revenue exists.

Subtitles as an asset

  • Subtitles are produced by contracted translators plus AI. Translation rights belong to KRD PLY and can be resold to distributors.
  • Rights are bought per territory: the Kurdistan Region and Iraq, plus “Kurdish-language” rights for the diaspora. Classic, independent and festival films have realistic regional prices; studio blockbusters are not an early target.
  • Links with KRD ART (animation, music videos) and KRD KID (children's content).

Subtitle pipeline

1 · Transcribe

Kurdish ASR produces a timed transcript. Source-language subtitles (SRT) are ingested for foreign films.

2 · Translate

MT into each dialect, using a glossary for names, idioms and cultural terms.

3 · Human QA

Paid native reviewers and a community guild, with reviewer credits shown on the title.

4 · Deliver

SRT is converted to WebVTT/IMSC with proper bidi for RTL, SDH captions for the deaf and hard of hearing, and user styling.

Search & transliteration

Search normalises across scripts: a query typed in Latin hesen zîrek finds Sorani حەسەن زیرەک and vice-versa. This requires a Kurdish-aware analyser (character folding, Arabic–Latin transliteration, dialect synonym lists) on the search engine. It is one of vyv's hardest and most defensible technical assets.

20 AI Governance & Ethics

Trustworthy by construction.

Frameworks

Aligned with the EU AI Act (transparency, risk management), ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system) and NIST AI RMF.

Transparency

Users can see why something was recommended. AI-generated content is labelled. AI agent chats say plainly that the user is talking to AI.

Human authority

No irreversible action (deletion, takedown, payout change, rights decision) happens without a human approver. Agents propose; people decide.

Cultural fairness

Recommendation diversity quotas across dialects, regions and gender, so that no part of Kurdish culture is algorithmically silenced. Bias audits are published every year.

AI policies

  • Every AI-produced datum carries a source label and a confidence level; no historical “fact” is published without a source.
  • Model training on the catalogue happens only with explicit opt-in and a revenue share for rights holders.
  • A complete audit log is kept for every change to rights or credit metadata.
  • Each agent has a stated autonomy level: fully automatic, automatic with sampled review, or human approval required.

24 Security, Privacy & Compliance

Security

Zero-trust networking, OWASP ASVS L2, secrets in vault, encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest, least-privilege IAM, yearly pen-tests and a bug bounty.

GDPR (EU users), data minimisation, consent management, export and delete-my-data, no sale of personal data, and a privacy-preserving analytics option.

Certifications path

ISO/IEC 27001 in year 2, SOC 2 Type II for enterprise and government, ISO/IEC 42001 for AI, and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility conformance.

30 Material, Motion & Icons

Liquid glass

Surface = translucent fill (5–15%) + backdrop-filter: blur(24–32px) saturate(170%) + 1 px hairline + inset top highlight + soft deep shadow. Three elevations: base, raised (cards, dock), overlay (sheets, menus).

Ease-out cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) for UI and a spring curve for playful moments. Durations: 150 ms (press), 250 ms (state), 450–600 ms (page). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

34 Fair Royalty Model

Artists first, visibly.

User-centric / artist-centric payouts

Each subscriber's fee is distributed to the artists that subscriber actually listened to (UCPS), with a boost for professional and heritage artists. This avoids the pro-rata model where a few global hits absorb everyone's money.

Transparent statements

Real-time dashboards, monthly statements, split sheets, and payouts to local wallets and IBAN.

Kurdish Rights Registry

vyv helps establish a neutral registry of works, recordings and rights-holders. It can seed a future Kurdish collective management organisation (CMO) for performance and mechanical rights.

Heritage Fund

A fixed share of revenue (target 2–5%) funds archive digitisation and payments to estates of historical artists.

Rights & Copyright

The biggest risk is also the deepest moat.

Every piece of music carries two separate rights, and vyv must license both. Because Iraq and the Kurdistan Region have no working collective management organisation, vyv becomes the first registry of Kurdish works and rights, a service it can also offer to government.

Master · the recording

Owned by the performer, label or producer. It covers streaming, download and sync of this specific recording.

Composition · the work

Owned by the composer and lyricist (publishing). It covers the melody and the words in any recording or performance.

Catalogue classes

ClassSourceRights model
Contemporary, directKurdish artists and labelsNon-exclusive digital licence; 60–70% of net revenue to rights holders
Global distributionAggregators and international labelsLicence with standard DDEX reporting
Heritage with heirsFamilies, archives, historic broadcastersHeirs' contract + revenue share + Heritage Fund
Orphan worksRecordings with no traceable ownerDocumented diligent search, publish with notice, pay into escrow until the owner appears, remove at once on objection
Public domainExpired worksFree, with full credit

Orphan-works flow

1 · Diligent search

Documented search of registries, heirs and archives.

2 · Publish with notice

Public notice and a claim button on every orphan work.

3 · Escrow

Royalties accrue in a separate escrow account.

4 · Pay or remove

Paid out when the owner is found; removed immediately on objection.

Principles

  • Internal work and recording IDs mapped to ISRC and ISWC.
  • A fast, public notice-and-takedown procedure, with a quarterly transparency page.
  • Monthly revenue statements in every artist's dashboard; payouts to local wallets.
  • Content and data are never used for political or surveillance purposes; neutrality in every recommendation.

This chapter is strategic guidance, not legal advice. Contracts must be drafted with intellectual-property counsel in the Kurdistan Region and the EU.

Governance & Independence

Public money, independent voice.

Strategic warning: the KRD holding charter rests on independence, with no government or party budget, above all for KRD MAG. Government capital in vyv could put that principle in question. The charter already contains the answer: ring-fencing.

Government · PPP

Buys the service “national digital archive” and sponsors Heritage. An optional minority stake carries no content veto.

KRD PLY · independent entity

Capital, public or private, enters at PLY level, never at the holding or MAG level.

Private investors

Telcos (carrier billing, data bundles), banks and wallets, diaspora investors, international cultural funds.

Independent content committee

Artists, researchers and every region. It decides selection and ranking; no shareholder can intervene.

Heritage Trust

Owns the preservation masters in public trust.

KRD holding

Brand endorsement, shared KRD ID and infrastructure, with no editorial control.

vyv Independence Charter

  • No shareholder intervenes in the selection, ranking or removal of content.
  • Algorithmic neutrality, measured by a public dialect and region coverage metric.
  • Government participation is minority, without veto over editorial or algorithms.
  • Heritage is always free to listen; the archive is a public good.
